Projection within the lepton structure

The hypothesis: the charged leptons (e, μ, τ) form one structure, and projection happens inside that structure, not at any one energy. If so, the same shape should show up in their masses and in their couplings. These panels put the two views side by side and show where each one holds.

A · Mass view: Koide triangle

The three generations are the corners. √mass of e, μ, τ, normalized, is one point.

B · Coupling view: the 1/3 split

A charged lepton's interaction as a vector: electromagnetic vs weak (Z) strength.

C · Where each view holds: energy scale

The weak mixing angle sin²θW (MS-bar) changes with the energy it is read at. The 1/3 rule gives one fixed number.
