Origin lab · lattice QCD (pure gauge SU(3))

The gluon field on a 4D grid of space and time. Every link between neighbouring points holds a 3×3 matrix (an SU(3) colour rotation). The grid is updated by Monte Carlo with the Wilson action, the same method used in real lattice QCD, at toy size. Watch for two things: Wilson loops shrinking with area (quarks are confined), and the Polyakov loop leaving zero when the box is short in time and β is large (the gluon plasma).

Smoothing (APE)

steps10
α0.50
slice shows

Each step replaces every link by (1 − α) × itself + α × the average of the paths that go round it, projected back onto SU(3). Short-distance jitter averages out; long-distance structure stays. It's applied to a copy, for measuring: the simulation itself is never smoothed. The potential uses smoothing in space only (time links stay raw).

Each square is one lattice site; brightness is the action at that site, summed over the six planes through it (1 − Re Tr P/3). Pale = calm field, deep colour = strongly excited. The numbers are the per-plane average. "Smoothed field" redraws every 20 sweeps and is coloured relative to the slice's own maximum, so the lumps that survive smoothing show up even though their action is small. Raise β and the field calms everywhere; lower it and it gets rough.

Plaquette over sweeps

Orange = ⟨Re Tr P/3⟩ each sweep. Dashed lines: strong-coupling β/18 + β²/216 (good for β ≲ 2) and weak-coupling 1 − 2/β (good for large β). Real QCD sits between them, near β 5.7–6.5.

Polyakov loop

–

One dot per spatial site: Tr of the time links all the way round the box, /3. The cloud's centre is ⟨L⟩. Confined: the cloud sits on 0 (a lone quark costs infinite energy). Deconfined: it moves out along one of the three marked Z₃ directions.

Quark–antiquark potential V(R)

Energy of a static quark and antiquark R apart, from smoothed Wilson loops: aV(R) = ln[W(R,T)/W(R,T+1)]. Confinement = V keeps rising in a straight line; the slope is the string tension σa². Orange line: Cornell fit V₀ + σR − e/R (R ≥ 2). Dashed: e fixed at π/12, the value for a vibrating string. Bars = jackknife errors. If Nt < 8, the loops use z as "time", and in the hot plasma that spatial potential still rises: real physics (the magnetic sector stays confining), not a bug.
