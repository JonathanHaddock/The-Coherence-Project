PART A: per-generation, mass-free quantities

  Z -> l l branching (%)               charged leptons  D = 3.02e-07   (LEP; e, mu, tau)
  Weak coupling ratios (tau decays)    charged leptons  D = 7.12e-07   (HFLAV: g_mu/g_e, g_tau/g_e)
  Anomalous magnetic moment a          charged leptons  not measurable per generation (a_e, a_mu measured; a_tau only bounded)
  Electric charge                      charged leptons  D = 0.00e+00   (|Q| = 1 for e, mu, tau)
  Electric charge                      neutrinos        not measurable per generation (0 for all three: no direction to project)
  Z -> nu nu per flavour               neutrinos        not measurable per generation (only the total is measured (N_nu = 2.996))
  Weak isospin |T3|                    neutrinos        D = 0.00e+00   (same for every generation)
  Weak isospin |T3|                    charged leptons  D = 0.00e+00   (same for every generation)

  Every measured per-generation quantity gives D ~ 0 (lepton universality):
  the generations are identical once mass and mixing are removed. Projected
  this way, charged leptons are pure identity too, the same as neutrinos.
